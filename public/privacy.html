Digger privacy

Applies to the game at pikos.org/onni/digger. Last updated 11 October 2026.

Playing without signing in

You can play without an account. Your own best score, deepest dig and sound setting are kept only in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional and only used to put your best score on the shared leaderboard. Google shares your name, profile picture, email address and a stable account ID with the game. The server checks the sign-in with Google and stores only your Google account ID, display name, profile picture link, best score, depth, drill level and the date of that score. Your email address is not stored.

Your name, picture and score are shown publicly on the leaderboard. Nothing is shared with anyone else, and the game does not use advertising or tracking.

Removing your data

Email onni.vierinen@gmail.com and your leaderboard entry will be deleted. Signing out in the game removes the sign-in from your browser.
